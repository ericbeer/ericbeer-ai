The 5-Minute Follow-Up Kit: AI prompts so no lead goes cold

Most leads don't say no. They just stop hearing from you. Somebody fills out your form, books nothing, and by the time you call back they've talked to someone else.

You don't need to hire someone to answer faster. You need AI to write every follow-up before the lead ever comes in, and your CRM to send it the second they do. Here are the exact prompts I use. Paste them into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI.

1

Step 1: Find out how fast you really answer

Fill out your own form right now, from your phone, like a stranger would. Start a timer. Write down how long it takes to get a reply, and what that reply says. Then do the same for every place leads come from: your site, your ads, your DMs, your phone line.

Then have AI map it for you:

Copy this inI run a business that helps [who] with [what]. My leads come from [list every source: website form, Facebook ads, Instagram DMs, phone calls, referrals]. For each source, ask me one question at a time: how fast we reply today, who replies, and what we say. Then give me a table of every source, its reply time, and the one fix that would make the biggest difference.
2

Step 2: Write the first reply before the lead arrives

The first message has one job: get a reply. Not a sales pitch. A short, human text with one easy question.

Copy this inWrite 3 versions of a first text message to someone who just asked about [your service] on [where they came from]. Rules: under 40 words, sounds like a real person not a company, uses their first name, mentions what they asked about, and ends with one simple question they can answer in a few words. No links, no emojis, no exclamation points. Then write a matching email version with a short subject line.

Pick the best one and load it into your CRM so it sends automatically the moment the form comes in. Not in an hour. Instantly.

3

Step 3: Write the next 7 days

Most people send one message and give up. Have AI write the whole week now, so following up takes zero willpower.

Copy this inA lead asked about [your service] and hasn't replied to my first text. Write a 7-day follow-up plan: day 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7. Mix texts and short emails. Each message gives them one new reason to talk to me: a quick tip, a common mistake, a short client story, a simple question. Every message is under 60 words, sounds like me ([describe how you talk: direct, warm, funny, plain]), and ends with one easy question. The last message is a polite check-in that makes it easy to say "not now."
4

Step 4: Wake up the leads you already paid for

Open your CRM and pull everyone from the last 90 days who never booked or bought. You already paid to get them. One short, honest text brings a surprising number back.

Copy this inWrite 3 short texts to people who asked about [your service] in the last 90 days and never booked. Under 30 words. No guilt, no pressure. Sound like a person checking in, for example "Hey [name], are you still looking for help with [problem]?" Give me one version that's a simple question, one that offers something useful, and one that gives them an easy way to say no.

Send it in small batches during business hours, and only to people who gave you permission to text them.

5

Step 5: Make it run without you

You don't need new software. Almost every CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Keap and others) can do this today:

  1. Trigger: a new lead comes in from any form.
  2. Instantly: send the first text and the email from Step 2.
  3. Then: run the 7-day plan from Step 3, and stop it the moment they reply.
  4. When they reply: a real person, you or your team, takes it from there.

The rule: AI writes and sends the follow-ups. A human answers the replies. Not sure how to set it up in your CRM? Ask:

Copy this inI use [your CRM]. Give me step-by-step instructions, click by click, to build an automation that: sends a text and an email the moment a new lead fills out my form, sends a follow-up on days 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7, stops as soon as the lead replies, and notifies me when they do. Assume I'm not technical.

The Claude Skills Starter Kit: 5 skills to install first so Claude does the work you keep doing yourself

You're a business owner using Claude without skills? Then you're still doing work it could do for you. Every time you explain how you want a proposal written, how you prep for a sales call, or how you close out the month, you're teaching Claude the same job again.

A skill fixes that. It teaches Claude one job, once, so you never explain it twice. These are the five I'd install first, with exactly where to click and a prompt to try each one. It takes about ten minutes. I checked every step against the app in November 2026. Claude moves its menus around often, so if something isn't where I say it is, use the search box at the top of Settings.

1

What a skill is (30 seconds)

A skill is a set of instructions Claude loads when a job calls for it. Think of it as a trained employee for one task. You install it once. After that you ask in plain words, and Claude uses the skill without you pointing to it.

  • A skill teaches Claude one job.
  • A plugin is a bundle of skills for a whole area, such as marketing or sales. You install it the same way.
  • A connector is different. It lets Claude into your other apps, such as Gmail or your calendar. Skills are the know-how. Connectors are the access.
2

How to install any skill

  1. Click your name in the bottom-left corner, then Settings.
  2. Click Customize.
  3. Click Skills at the top.
  4. Click Discover. Yours shows what you already have. Discover shows what you can add.
  5. Find the skill, or type its name in the search box, and click the + next to it.

There's a Try button next to most of them if you want to test one before you add it. Anything you add moves to Yours, where you can turn it off or remove it later.

Check who made it. The directory has thousands of skills from many authors. Every one in this guide is from Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, and says so on its card. For anything else, read who it's by before you add it.

If you don't see Skills or the +. Some of this depends on your plan, and Claude rolls features out in stages. Use the ones you can see and come back for the rest.

3

Skill 1: Small Business

What it does. Ready-made workflows to help you run and grow your business. It works with most of the tools you already use, and you approve each step before it happens.

  1. Open Settings, then Customize, then Skills.
  2. Type Small Business in the search box at the top.
  3. Find the one that says Small Business, by Anthropic and click the + next to it.

It's also under Most installed skills on the Discover page.

Try itI own [business name], a [what the business does] with [team size] people. Use the Small Business skill. What are the three workflows you'd set up for me first, and what do you need from me to start the first one?
4

Skill 2: Marketing

What it does. Creates content, plans campaigns and looks at what's working across your marketing channels. It also helps keep your brand voice consistent.

  1. Open Settings, then Customize, then Skills.
  2. Type Marketing in the search box at the top.
  3. Find the one that says Marketing, Plugin, from Anthropic and click the + next to it.

This one is a plugin, so you get several marketing skills in one install.

Try itPlan a two-week campaign for [offer or product]. My customers are [who they are]. I post on [channels]. Give me the plan as a calendar, then write the first three posts.
5

Skill 3: Sales

What it does. Preps you for calls, summarizes them, writes the follow-ups and helps you review your deals.

  1. Open Settings, then Customize, then Skills.
  2. Type Sales in the search box at the top.
  3. Find the one that says Sales, Plugin, from Anthropic and click the + next to it.

Also a plugin: several sales skills in one install.

Try itI have a call tomorrow with [name, company] about [what you sell]. Prep me: what to ask, what they probably care about, and the objections I should expect. Then draft the follow-up email I'll send after the call.
6

Skill 4: Finance

What it does. Helps with finance and accounting work: journal entries, reconciliation, financial statements and explaining why the numbers moved.

  1. Open Settings, then Customize, then Skills.
  2. Type Finance in the search box at the top.
  3. Find the one that says Finance, by Anthropic and click the + next to it.

It's the most installed skill in the directory right now.

Try itHere is last month's profit and loss and the month before: [paste or attach both]. Tell me in plain words what changed, what's worth worrying about, and the three questions I should ask my bookkeeper.

It works from the numbers you give it, so check them, and have your accountant sign off on anything you file.

7

Skill 5: Skill Creator

What it does. This is the one that makes the other four look small. You tell Claude how you do a job, and it builds the skill for you. After that, Claude does that job your way every time.

  1. Open Settings, then Customize, then Skills.
  2. Look under Yours first. If skill-creator is already there, you have it.
  3. If not, type skill-creator in the search box, find the one from Anthropic and click the +.
Try itUse the skill creator to build me a skill for [a job you repeat, for example: writing a quote for a new customer]. Here's how I do it today: [describe the steps in your own words]. Here are two examples of ones I've done: [paste them]. Ask me whatever you need before you build it.

What to turn into a skill first. Pick a job you do every week, that you do the same way each time, and that you've explained to Claude more than once. Quotes, weekly reports, replies to the same customer questions and meeting follow-ups are all good ones.

8

The five-minute check

Open a new chat and run these. Each one tells you whether a step worked.

Checks what's installedWhich skills do you have available right now? List them with one line on what each one is for.
Checks a skill in useDraft a follow-up email to a customer who went quiet after I sent a quote for [what you quoted].

In the second one, watch for Claude naming the skill it used. If it didn't use one, say use the Sales skill once. You can also type / in the chat box to see your skills and pick one.

Your First 5 AI Jobs: stop doing the work AI should be doing

Most business owners use AI like a smarter Google. Ask a question, copy the answer, then still do the next five steps yourself.

The shift is to give AI a job: a repeating piece of work it owns from start to finish, and only comes to you when a human is needed. Here's how to find your first five.

1

Step 1: Write down your real week

For one week, jot down every task you or your team repeats. Don't filter. Follow-ups, emails, proposals, posts, reports, scheduling, data entry. Or skip the writing and let AI interview you:

Copy this inI run a business that helps [who] with [what]. Interview me, one question at a time, about everything my team and I repeat every week. When you're done, list every repeating task with how often it happens and roughly how long it takes.
2

Step 2: Score each task with 4 questions

A task is a great AI job when you can answer yes to most of these:

  • Does it repeat? Weekly or more is ideal.
  • Are the steps mostly the same each time?
  • Is the information already somewhere? An email, a call recording, your CRM, a form.
  • Is a mistake cheap to fix? Start where a slip costs a minute, not a client.
3

Step 3: The 5 jobs most businesses start with

  1. Lead follow-up: knows who the lead is and what happened on the call, writes the follow-up, updates the CRM, and flags you only when a human is needed.
  2. Inbox sorting and reply drafts: sorts what matters and drafts replies in your voice for your OK.
  3. Meeting prep and recap: a one-page brief before each call, action items after.
  4. Content repurposing: one video or post turned into captions, emails and short posts.
  5. Weekly numbers report: the numbers you check every week, in one short summary.

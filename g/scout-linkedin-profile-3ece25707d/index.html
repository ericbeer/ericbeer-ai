Let Claude rewrite your whole LinkedIn profile: connect Scout and press one button

My LinkedIn profile got hacked. Someone filled it with jobs I never had, at Boeing and Lockheed, and a university I never went to. On top of that, I hadn't really updated it in years, because rewriting your own profile is a chore nobody wants.

So I let Claude do it. I connected a tool called Scout to Claude, gave it my resume, and pressed one button. A team of AI agents read my background, checked every line against what's actually true, and rewrote my whole profile: headline, About, experience, Featured and Skills.

Here's exactly how to do the same thing, step by step, with a screenshot of every screen. It takes about 15 minutes.

One honest note before you start: Scout writes everything, and you paste it into LinkedIn yourself. Scout never logs into LinkedIn or posts for you, because LinkedIn bans automated activity and your account is worth protecting. Copying a few sections in takes a couple of minutes.

1

Step 1: Get Scout (free)

Scout is the AI job agent that does the work. It's built by BeChosen and lives at scoutjob.ai. Your CareerID (the profile Scout builds about you) is free, and the paid tools come with a 7-day trial, no card needed.

Go to scoutjob.ai and click Start free.

While you're getting set up, grab two files you'll give Scout in Step 4:

  1. Your resume (PDF or Word).
  2. Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF. On LinkedIn, open your profile, click Resources (or More), then Save to PDF.
2

Step 2: Connect Scout to Claude

Now plug Scout into Claude. This works on claude.ai and in the Claude desktop app.

  1. Click Customize in the left sidebar, then Connectors.
  1. Click Add in the top right, then Add custom connector.
  1. Name it Scout Jobs. For the URL, paste this exactly:
Paste this URLhttps://scoutjob.ai/mcp
  1. Leave everything else as it is (no client ID or secret needed) and click Continue.
  2. When it asks, sign in with your BeChosen account and tap Allow.

You'll see Scout in your connectors list, marked Connected.

Custom connectors depend on your Claude plan. If you don't see Add custom connector, check Claude's help page on custom connectors for your plan.

3

Step 3: Let Scout work without asking every time

One more setting, and it matters. Open Scout in your connectors list. Under Tool permissions, set the groups to Always allow.

That lets Scout work for you without stopping to ask permission at every step. Anything that sends, applies, publishes or charges money still needs your own tap on a scoutjob.ai page, so you stay in control.

4

Step 4: Give Scout your resume

Open a new chat in Claude, attach your resume and your LinkedIn PDF, and say hi.

Copy this inHi Scout. Here's my resume and my LinkedIn profile as a PDF. Get to know me and build my CareerID. The job I want next is [the role or kind of role you want].

Scout reads everything and builds your CareerID: a detailed profile of your background, your results, and what's actually true about you. Everything else Scout does is checked against it. That's why the fake Boeing and Lockheed jobs on my profile didn't survive: they weren't in my CareerID, so Scout flagged them instead of keeping them.

Ask for the menu any time:

Copy this inScout, show me the menu
5

Step 5: Press the one button

In the menu, under Prepare your materials, tap Recruiters find me on LinkedIn. (Or just type Start: Recruiters find me on LinkedIn.)

This is the one button. Behind it, a LinkedIn Profile manager runs a team of specialist agents:

  • Resume Reader takes in your resume, your LinkedIn PDF and the jobs you want.
  • Role Analyst pulls the words recruiters actually search for in those jobs.
  • Profile Auditor checks your current profile against those jobs before changing anything.
  • Positioner decides who your profile says you are.
  • Proof Selector picks the best proof for each section.
  • Profile Writer writes your headline, About, experience, Featured and Skills.
  • Claim Checker checks every line against your CareerID, so nothing untrue gets in.
  • Activity Planner plans 4 weeks of posts that show what you know.
  • Outreach Writer drafts endorsement asks for you to send.

You get back every section, ready to paste.

6

Step 6: Paste it into LinkedIn

Open your LinkedIn profile in another tab and work top to bottom: headline, About, each job in Experience, Featured, then Skills. Click the pencil on each section and paste in what Scout wrote.

If something doesn't sound like you, tell Scout. It rewrites from your CareerID, so it stays true.

Copy this inScout, my About section sounds too formal. Rewrite it the way I talk: [paste a sentence or two you'd actually say].
Copy this inScout, give me 3 more headline options aimed at [the role you want], under 220 characters.

If your profile was tampered with like mine, delete anything Scout flagged as not matching your CareerID, then change your LinkedIn password and turn on two-step verification.

7

Keep it current from now on

Your profile goes stale because updating it is a chore. With Scout it isn't. When something changes, tell Scout in one line and it keeps your CareerID current:

Copy this inScout, here's what happened today: [new project, new result, new certification, new role].

Then run Recruiters find me on LinkedIn again whenever you want the profile refreshed. Scout also posts nothing for you: the 4 weeks of posts it plans are drafts you copy and post yourself.

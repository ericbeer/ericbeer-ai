The AI System Starter Kit: 3 prompts that turn AI into a system

Most people use AI like Google. Ask a question, get an answer, close the tab. The value is when AI helps you build systems that do real work in your business, every day, without starting from zero each time.

You don't need to be techy. Use these in ChatGPT, Claude, or whatever AI you like, in order.

1

Prompt 1: Find the problem worth solving

Don't start with the tool. Start with the outcome.

Copy this inI own a business that helps [who you help] with [what you help them do]. Ask me questions, one at a time, about what my team and I do every week: getting leads, following up, creating content, answering emails, proposals, onboarding, reporting, customer service, and anything else that takes time. When you're done asking, give me a list of the 5 tasks AI could take over or speed up the most, ranked by how much time and money they'd save me. For each one, tell me why.

Pick the top one. That's your first system.

2

Prompt 2: Make the AI ask you the right questions

Most bad AI answers come from vague prompts. Flip it. Let the AI figure out what it needs to know.

Copy this inI have a business that helps [who] solve [problem]. I want to use AI to automate and solve this specific problem: [the task from Prompt 1], so that [the result you want]. Before you tell me how to do it, tell me what questions I should answer so you can give me the best possible plan. Ask them one at a time.

Answer every question. Short answers are fine.
